# Mentorship area: agent rules

## Language: English allowed

Unlike the root [AGENTS.md](/AGENTS.md), which requires **Mongolian** for all user-facing text and content across the site, **mentorship content and UI may be in English**.

- This area is aimed at **professional/technical** use (mentorship topics, developer guidance, tooling, etc.).
- English is often clearer and more precise for technical and professional material.
- When working in `app/mentorship/` and related mentorship content, prefer English for topic titles, descriptions, MDX body text, and any mentorship-specific UI—unless a specific piece is intentionally in Mongolian.

The root rule still applies elsewhere on the site: general blog, calculators, navigation, and other user-facing content remain Mongolian.
